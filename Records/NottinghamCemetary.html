Burial Records for Cullen in
Nottingham General Cemetary

Contributed by Andy Johnson of Lancashire, England

Many thanks to Andy Johnson for taking the time to send this information from his research notes. Andy's interest in the Cullen family concerns the descendants of William & Elizabeth (Richards) Cullen of Radford, Nottinghamshire. His family page on this site is located in the family history section of the Records Archive. These entries for the Cullens found in the Nottingham General Cemetary (1838-present) are ordered alphabetically but you may want to take note of the grave numbers as they will provide some clues to the family groups buried together.

Below the list is a table which contains the same information except that it is ordered by grave number and notes have been added for the individuals who have been identified. This is an ongoing project and anyone who can help is encouraged to forward what you know of these Cullens buried in Nottingham General Cemetary.

Recent Additions and/or Corrections

  • Grave 1025 - Benjamin died 29 Dec 1891; Mary Anne d 28 Mar 1860; Sarah d 8 Nov 1917; Benjamin d 15 Aug 1867 was 3rd son of Benjamin & Mary; Esther Isabella d 27 Nov 1870 eldest daughter of Benjamin & Mary; Mary Ann d 28 March 1893 2nd dau of Benjamin & Mary. Also Fanny Elizabeth d 25 June 1903 age 37 3rd dau of Benjamin & Mary.
  • Grave 1032 is William Ekins not Perkins. He died 27 April 1882.
  • Grave 1245 - Elizabeth d 28 Feb 1883; Marianne d 28 Sept 1900; Charles d 11 April 1901. Also Sarah ROBERTS, wife of William ROBERTS of Acacia House Beeston d 6 Aug 1887.
  • Grave 3898 - Fanny d 20 Sept 1924.
  • Grave 8861/8848/8836 - Thomas d 20 Jan 1888; Sarah d 9 Nov 1869; Thomas Augustus d 29 Oct 1870; Edward Marshall d 25 Jan 1867 husband of Rosamond; Elizabeth Rowena d 16 Nov 1902; Arthur Edward d 19 Aug 1881 born 22 April 1856; Mary Elizabeth d 10 Dec 1871 born Dec25 1854; Florence Ada d 18 Dec 1874 b July 17 1866; Thomas KNIGHT d 3 March 1889; Mary Ann KNIGHT d 28 Nov 1868 wife of Thomas KNIGHT.Also Thomas Knight (Cullen) d 16 March 1904 in Cape Town age 50 son of Thomas Augustus; Rosamond (Cullen) d 18 Aug 1864 age 34 wife of Edward Marshall interred at Shardlow, Derbyshire; Augustus Henry (Cullen) d Easter Sunday 1918 in Manchester b 6 Dec 1861; James Harris (Cullen) d 14 Sept 1919 in Niue (South Pacific) b 20 Nov 1864.
  • Grave 4721/4760 : Alfred Cullen d 22 Sept 1924 aged 73, his wife Elizabeth d 3 Feb 1937 aged 86, their sons John Wilkinson Cullen d Perth Western Australia 1 Feb 1926 aged 37 & Lieutenant William Harold Cullen killed in action Mametz Wood France 11 July 1916 age 26. Also John Cullen d 4 June 1898 aged 88 & his wife Ann Wilkinson d 23 June 1886 aged 73. Also Alfred Edgar Cullen d Perth, WA 24 Dec 1948 aged 65.
  • Grave 6448 : William Cullen d 29 Apr 1901, his wife Sarah Anne d 21 Aug 1920 & their daughter Mary Eliza d 30 Nov 1877.
  • Grave 6449 : Albert Henry Cullen died 20 Dec 1941, his wife Frances d 23 Dec 1930 & their youngest son Hubert Ernest (Bertie) d 4 Dec 1907 age 8.
  • Grave 6479 : John Charles Cullen died 3 Aug 1932 & his wife Rebecca died 22 Dec 1884 age 27 (born 1857 not age 39 born 1845).
  • Grave 20718 : Frederick Cullen d 26 July 1887, his wife Elizabeth d 6 Feb 1890 aged 64 & their daughter Clara d 13 Sept 1879 aged 18.
