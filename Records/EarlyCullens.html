Early Cullens of Upton Nottinghamshire
Miscellaneous Records Prior to 1600

Jim Cullen

This page will hold the miscellaneous records I've come across that demonstrate that there were Cullen families living in central Nottinghamshire prior to about 1600. These references will be little more than scraps with minimal information given to go along with them. These families, unknown until now, were contemporaries of Richard Cullen who died in Upton in 1580. While connections to the Cullens of Upton and surrounding areas are not known, it can be assumed there is some connection in some cases. On the other hand, there are known records of very early Cullens in neighboring Lincolnshire that I don't mention much on this site since there is just no evidence yet for their connection to the known Cullen families rooted in the Village of Upton.

Transcriptions

Nuncuperative Will of Margaret Robinson Feb 8, 1591

This is a nuncupative will of Margaret Robinson in the probate court of Southwell written Feb 8, 1591 and probated Mar 18, 1591. It seems that Margaret may have suffered a sudden illness at a relatively young age based on the type of will and the state of the family. Margaret was a "spinster" and "late of Upton, Southwell". The appointed Executrix was her mother, Katherine Robinson. Now Robinson was a well known and prominent name is Upton in those days. The list of witnesses were: Robert Gibson, Thomas Cullen, Katherine Robinson, and Henrie Robinson. Now, it's not known if this Thomas Cullen is the son of Richard Cullen who died in 1580 in Upton but, if it was, he was about 25 years of age at the time. Now we come to the mentioned family members which is the really interesting part. Henrie Robinson is Margaret's brother. Marie Gibson is Margaret's sister. Marie "has family" which I take to mean is married to a Gibson, probably Robert Gibson in the list of witnesses. Five Gibson children are mentioned for this marriage. Margaret also had a brother Robert Cullen who was old enough at the time to have a son, John Cullen, who was also mentioned in the will. This puts Robert at the very least 21 years of age and so born prior to about 1570, making Robert a contemporary of Richard Cullen. There is no mention of a Robert Cullen in Richard's family so he must have been a nephew or possibly a cousin, if he was indeed related as we suspect. Margaret's mother Katherine (maiden name unknown) must have first married a Cullen, having at least the son Robert, then later married again to a Robinson and having at least three children Margaret, Henrie, and Marie. Note that we can't be certain if Marie was a Cullen or a Robinson - it's assumed that, since she was older with a family of her own, it may be more likely that she was a Cullen. Even though Margaret's mother Katherrine was named as Executrix, after Margaret's death, her brother Henry Robinson was appointed to handle Margaret's estate and affairs. The reason for this could be that Margaret's mother Katherine was also ill, possibly from some disease that had struck the family. We see that immediately after the probate of Margaret Robinson, her mother Katherine died intestate and her probate appears on Mar 24, 1591. She is recorded as a widow late of Oxton, Nottinghamshire. Her son Henry Robinson was appointed to handle her estate and affairs. Being a widow at the time of her death, she outlived her second husband.
